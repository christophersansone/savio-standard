# The Family Covenant

*The Savio Standard · One page, signed as a household, renewed each school year*

---

As parents at **[School]**, we believe our children are made in the image of God, and that how they treat one another is the truest measure of what we are teaching them. We therefore commit:

1. **The standard.** We hold the Golden Rule as the standard of conduct for our children and ourselves — not an aspiration, but a standard.

2. **Humility.** We accept that our child is capable of unkindness, exclusion, and cruelty, as all children are. If another parent approaches us about our child's behavior, our first response will be to listen, thank them, and seek the truth — not to deny, dismiss, or retaliate.

3. **Directness.** If we have a concern about another covenant family's child, we will bring it privately, charitably, and promptly to those parents — never to the bleachers, the group chat, or the rumor mill — according to Christ's teachings in Matthew 18.

4. **Teaching the standard.** We will actively teach our children to include the excluded, to refuse to be an audience for cruelty, and to speak up or seek help when they see someone mistreated.

5. **Accountability at home.** When our child harms another, we will require of them genuine repair: apology, restitution, and changed behavior. We will follow through on consequences at home even when the school does not.

6. **Digital oversight.** We will treat technology access as earned, not entitled. Whatever devices our children use will be filtered, supervised, and age-appropriate. Their messaging will be open to our review at any time, and they will know it. Devices will stay out of bedrooms overnight, and we will not provide our children with unrestricted, unsupervised access to technology.

7. **Presence.** We will show up — to homeroom and grade gatherings, to formation nights when we can, and for one another's families in times of difficulty.

8. **Prayer.** We will pray regularly for the children of this school by name and grade — including, especially, the ones who are hardest to love.

## How signing works

**Your signature is private until your grade activates.** When eight families in the same grade have signed, all eight are notified and introduced to one another. Until then, no one knows you have signed. This is deliberate: no family should ever feel alone in pursuing what is good. Once a grade activates, new signers join the activated group immediately.

**Signing is free, and always will be.** There are no dues, no fees, and nothing to buy — see the [Stewardship Commitment](../docs/stewardship-commitment.md).

**The covenant is renewed each school year.** Renewal is a choice, made freshly, every fall. A covenant signed once and forgotten is a signed PDF; a covenant renewed annually is a practice.

**The covenant binds only its signers.** It judges no one, names no one, and asks nothing of any family that has not signed it. It is sustained by nothing except conscience and community — which is the point.
