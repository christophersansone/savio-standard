# The Savio Standard

*A parent-led movement to re-orient culture in Catholic grade schools*

> "So in everything, do to others what you would have them do to you, for this sums up the Law and the Prophets." — Matthew 7:12

## What it is

The Savio Standard is a return to the Golden Rule as the standard of Christian community: not the exception we celebrate when we see it, but the baseline every family lives and expects.

Named for St. Dominic Savio, the schoolboy saint who organized his classmates to defend the smallest among them, The Savio Standard is a lay movement of Catholic families choosing to take Christ's command seriously and build the structures necessary to live it together.

## Why it exists

Everything left unattended drifts toward disorder: a garden, a house, a marriage, a company. Culture is no exception. Whatever is merely accepted becomes the norm, and the norm eventually becomes the goal.

Catholic schools already share the same mission: forming children who know they are loved and learn to love others well. What is often missing is not the destination, but the shared framework for getting there. The Savio Standard exists to fill that gap: not by changing the goal, but by giving Catholic communities a shared framework to reach it together.

## How it works

Every Catholic school family is already on the same team. What has been missing is the shared framework to move together: the standard everyone can point to, and the infrastructure that puts it into daily practice.

**The standard.** The Family Covenant translates the Golden Rule into eight concrete commitments, households promising how they will hold themselves accountable and how they will treat one another.

**The infrastructure.**

- **A shared language.** A common vocabulary for naming conflict, exclusion, and cruelty clearly, instead of waving it off as "drama."
- **A parent-to-parent protocol.** A direct, charitable way to raise a concern between families, backed by trained volunteer facilitators for the conversations that need a third party.
- **A formation series.** Ongoing sessions that teach parents the standard, the language, and the protocol, so the whole community is speaking the same terms.
- **Community roles.** Homeroom Hosts and School Leads who keep every family known, every gathering open to all, and no single volunteer carrying the whole load.
- **Digital oversight norms.** Shared household expectations for devices and access, so no family faces that decision alone.

Covenants activate grade by grade, so no family ever stands alone. It is free, it needs no one's permission, and it asks nothing the Gospel does not already ask.
