**Condensed :30 version (VO only, same visual logic compressed):**

> Catholic schools promise a community formed by the Gospel. But over time, kind stops being cool — teasing, gossip, exclusion, all waved off as "drama." And when a parent raises a concern, the answer is denial, not honesty.
>
> Christ already gave us the fix: love your neighbor, lived out through the Golden Rule. The Savio Standard reorients school culture around that — parents equipped with a covenant, specific commitments we pledge to live out, together.
>
> Visit saviostandard.org. Read it. Sign it.

Should feel like a coach's film-room breakdown, not a parody. The two **[Beat]** rows are deliberate silences — let the cut and the sound design do the work, don't rush past them.

**AI video-gen workflow notes:**

**Shared production notes:**
- All three scripts land on the same CTA construction: **saviostandard.org → read → sign** — keep this consistent across every cut so the site's "read the covenant, then sign it" funnel isn't fragmented.
- "Theme / rules / playbook" (Script B) and "till and keep" (Script C) are new framings (not pulled from existing project docs) — worth sanity-checking with anyone else on the team before either becomes a recurring motif elsewhere (merch, formation nights, etc.).
- Script B intentionally frames the "rules" as things kids are taught to live by, not just prohibitions to avoid — and pairs every "don't" with a "here's how we repair it," echoing the covenant's own Commitments 4 (Teaching the Standard) and 5 (Accountability at Home). Script C's covenant beat leans on the same idea from a different angle: kneeling beside a struggling family instead of walking past it.
- Script C deliberately avoids a denial/blame beat (an earlier draft had a "not my garden" line, cut because the analogy didn't hold) — the interdependence line ("a weed left in one plot... spreads to the row next door") carries that weight instead, without pointing fingers.
- All three scripts avoid naming "social aggression" or "DARVO" on-screen (too clinical for video) — that vocabulary lives in the glossary/protocol for people who click through.
- Scripts B and C both run long (:100–:130) by design — the emotional turns need room. If shorter cuts are needed later, trim the setup (football rules / till-keep definitions) before touching the turn or the antidote.
