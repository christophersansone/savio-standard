# The Savio Standard — Intro Video Script

*30–60 seconds · audience: Catholic school parents hearing about this for the first time*

---

## :60 VERSION

**[VISUAL: Kids on a playground — one child alone, others in a tight cluster nearby. Warm, real, not staged-sad.]**

**VO:** Every Catholic school shares the same mission: kids who know they're loved, and who learn to love others well.

**[VISUAL: Cut to a phone lighting up with a group chat notification. A parent's face, uncertain.]**

**VO:** But somewhere between the mission and the Tuesday afternoon group chat, something gets lost. Exclusion becomes "just drama." Cruelty becomes something we notice — but don't quite know how to name, or how to fix, without making it worse.

**[VISUAL: Text on screen — "Matthew 7:12" — then a hand signing a printed page.]**

**VO:** The Savio Standard is a return to something simpler: the Golden Rule, held not as a poster on the wall, but as the actual, expected standard of how our kids — and we — treat one another.

**[VISUAL: A small group of parents talking on a porch, warm light, easy conversation.]**

**VO:** It's a covenant — eight plain commitments — signed by families in the same grade. When enough families sign, they're introduced to each other. So no parent is holding this alone.

**[VISUAL: Laptop screen showing the website, cursor scrolling.]**

**VO:** It's free. It's parent-led. And it asks nothing the Gospel doesn't already ask.

**[VISUAL: Logo / St. Dominic Savio image. Text: saviostandard.org]**

**VO:** Visit saviostandard.org to read the covenant — and sign it for your family.

---

## :30 VERSION (tight cut)

**[VISUAL: Playground, one kid on the edges. Cut to a parent's uncertain face lit by a phone.]**

**VO:** Every Catholic school wants the same thing: kids who know they're loved, and learn to love others well. But too often, exclusion just gets waved off as "drama."

**[VISUAL: Hand signing a page. Small group of parents talking warmly.]**

**VO:** The Savio Standard is the Golden Rule, restored as an actual standard — eight commitments, signed by families in your child's grade, so no parent carries it alone.

**[VISUAL: Website on screen, then logo.]**

**VO:** It's free, parent-led, and asks nothing the Gospel doesn't already ask. Visit saviostandard.org to read the covenant and sign it for your family.

---

**Notes for production:**
- Keep VO warm and unhurried — this is an invitation, not an ad.
- Avoid depicting an identifiable "bullied kid"; keep visuals suggestive, not literal.
- CTA on screen should hold for at least 3 seconds: **saviostandard.org**
